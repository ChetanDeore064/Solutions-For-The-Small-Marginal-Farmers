Why we started

Most farmers in India work less than two hectares. They decide on seed, water and sale price with little data, and one bad guess can cost a whole season.

Advice, weather data and market prices exist, but they rarely reach these farmers in a form they can use. We built our tools to close that gap, working alongside farmers rather than just for them.

What we do

🌱

Crop advice

🔍

Disease detection
